# game-playground-maps

Custom sandbox maps for game-playground, as JSON documents. Read `../../CLAUDE.md` and `../game-playground/CLAUDE.md` first: the format is defined, validated and built by game-playground's `maps/pg_data.gd`.

**`maps/` is what the game reads**, through a dot-bootstrap link (`game-playground/.gitignore`: `# bootstrap-link: game-playground-maps/maps` → `/maps/custom`). `Playground.map_catalogue()` adds every `.json` there as a `DotMapDef` built by the shared `res://maps/pg_data.tscn`, with the document's path in `meta.doc`; a document that does not read is left out with a WARN, and an id the catalogue already has is never replaced.

**Why documents and not map scripts.** The built-in maps are `_build()` scripts. A map delivered in a pack of its own is mounted at another prefix than the game, and a script there that `extends` the game's map base by path names a path that is only right in one of the two places it runs (the family's mount constraint). Boxes are data: either pack can be anywhere.

**Materials.** A box may name a `material` (`mat(material, at, size)`): one of dot-physics' standard surfaces, drawn in its own look unless it names a colour; water and lava are liquid volumes with no collider.

**Props.** A document may list `props` (catalogue ids, frozen unless `loose`) and `wires` between them, which game-playground puts down on the server when the map loads (its CLAUDE.md, *A map document can carry props*). `prop()` takes the floor a prop stands on and adds half its height from `HALF_HEIGHT`; keep that table in step with game-playground's catalogue.

**`tools/build_maps.py` writes every document, and the JSON is committed.** Edit the builder, run it, commit both; `--check` fails when a committed file differs. Ramps are placed by their top surface's two ends and dropped along their normal by half their thickness, so the surface meets the ground exactly at both ends (dropped vertically, the top end is a lip, and over a step's height a lip is a wall).

## The maps

**Props added to the first three (2026-10-07).** Build Plots: a supply depot on the plaza (six planks, three panels, four crates, a barrel, all loose), trees where the paths cross, lamps on the plaza's corners, and a buggy by the deck. Quarry: a blasting hut on the west terrace whose door is wired to a lever outside, six barrels inside, two barrel clusters on the pit floor, ore crates by the cart, three boulders on the north rim one push from the edge, and a buggy by the spawn. Slide Hill: four sleds (planks at friction 0.05) on the deck at the slope's head (**measured**: one nudged at 4 m/s slides from the deck to the far wall, 20 m down), crates on the hill, balls in the halfpipe, and a buggy at the foot. A probe loading every map (now game-playground's `tools/map_props.gd`) found all 114 props placed and none moved more than 0.5 m in five seconds.

- **`pgc_plots` — Build Plots.** A 160 m walled plate: a spawn plaza in the middle, eight 26 m plots on a 40 m pitch round it, each a 0.2 m pad (a step) in its own colour with a 2 m claim pad on the path in front of it, and a viewing deck 4 m up in the north-west corner on a 20-degree ramp. After the plot-and-claim-pad layout of the 2026-10-05 reference video (`/extra/external/video/26-10-05/playground01.mp4`); claiming is drawn, not implemented.
- **`pgc_quarry` — Quarry.** A pit in two terraces (rim at 0, terrace at -6, floor at -12) with 6 m ramps between each level on the north and south sides, offset so the way down is a walk round; rails across the pit floor and along the north terrace (0.15 m, a step), a cart on the pit rails (1.0 m, a climb), three climbable ore piles and two 2.5 m boulders nobody climbs. After the mine-cart area in the same video.

- **`pgc_slopes` — Slide Hill.** A 180 m walled plate. A 14-degree hill 80 m long up to a 30 x 20 m deck 20 m up, with a pen on it (a 1 m wall round 8 m, under the 1.035 m a jump climbs, so nobody is shut in) to stand in and be picked up and thrown from, after the same video's "stand in the box" loop; and a 24-degree slope down the far side. **Measured, not assumed**: at the catalogue's friction (1.0) a plank or crate settles on the slope and stays (0.6-0.9 m, the drop onto it), so it can be built on; with friction 0.2, set by the tool gun's Edit properties or Physical properties, it slides about 35 m to the bottom in six seconds, and game-playground's prop surfing carries whoever stands on it. A 45-degree slope would slide everything at the default friction and nobody could walk it. On the east side a halfpipe: two 31-degree faces 14 m apart, each topped by a 5 m ledge 6 m up. `ramp_x` is `ramp_z`'s twin for ramps rising along X.

- **`pgc_town` — Town.** A 200 m walled town on grass. Two crossing roads 14 m wide (the north-south one a hair higher, so the crossing is one surface), five buildings on 0.15 m pavements. Each building has a doorway with a **door prop wired to a button outside and one inside** (F on the door works too), loose crates and a barrel inside, and a ramp up the side wall away from the road to a post level with the roof. The post touches the wall: the first version left 0.2 m between them, a slot. A fountain plaza has trees round it, a stage, three market stalls with goods, benches and beach balls. Street trees and lamps run down the north-south road, and a car park in the south-east corner holds two buggies. The first map to use the document's `props` and `wires`.

- **`pgc_islands` — Sky Islands.** A 240 m walled plate. Six 20 m islands on a 55 m ring, tops 8 to 33 m, joined in order by 4 m sloped bridges (`bridge()`, about 10 degrees, no rails). From the top island a 6 m red slide runs straight back down to the first, 25 m over 32 (about 38 degrees, walkable just under the 46 limit), and a ramp climbs from the ground to the first. Low-friction sleds and stacked crates sit on the islands, with boulders at the head of the slide and barrels on the third. **Measured**: a boulder nudged at 3 m/s rolls down the slide and comes to rest on the first island. A fall lands on the ground, which leads back to the ramp, so nowhere is a trap.

- **`pgc_site` — Construction Site.** A 180 m dirt plate inside a 3 m yellow fence. The centrepiece is a half-built frame: 30 x 20 m, three open floors every 4.5 m on 0.6 m columns. It is climbed by a switchback on its east side: lanes A and B, landings spanning both and touching each slab, and the third ramp 9 m over the first. The ramps start flush with the frame, because 1 m in left a 0.4 m slot against the corner column, which the survey found. Also: a tower crane (a 28 m mast and a 40 m jib over the frame, declared), a dirt kicker and a bumps track for the two buggies, a site office with a button-wired door and cones, and piles of planks, beams, panels, slabs and pillars to build with. Crates and a barrel sit up on the frame to throw off it.

- **`pgc_warehouse` — Warehouse.** The first indoor map. A 160 m yard holds an 80 x 50 m hall whose floor is at dock height: 1.2 m, a climb no jump makes. The hall has 12 m walls and a roof, and two 8 m loading doorways. The east doorway has a ramp up from the yard; the west one is a sheer dock edge to drive a buggy off. Inside: four 30 m rows of racking with shelves at 2.5 and 5 m holding forty loose crates (the shelves are declared), and a 2.5 m catwalk 6 m up along the north and east walls. The catwalk is reached by a ramp up the north wall and holds barrels to roll off. It starts exactly where its ramp tops out: overlapping the ramp's end left 1.4 m of headroom where they met, and the survey reported the whole catwalk unreached. An office with a button-wired door stands inside. The yard has shipping containers (some two high, tops declared), pallets of planks and two buggies.

- **`pgc_canyon` — Canyon.** Two 90 m mesas, 20 m high, either side of a 40 m canyon with a dry riverbed down it, inside 24 m walls (4 m over the mesas, so a thrown prop stays in). Each mesa is reached from the floor by a 60 m ramp flush against the canyon wall (18 degrees). Three crossings: a railed road bridge at z 0, a 1.2 m balance beam at z -60, and at z 60 a broken bridge. Its west half is a `bridge()` rising 3 m to a lip 12 m short of the flat east half. **Measured with a buggy launched off the lip**: at 12 m/s it falls into the canyon; at 16, 20 and 25 m/s it lands on the east half (the buggy tops out near 24). There are metre-high rocks on the mesas (a jump climbs them), six boulders on the rims one push from the edge, barrels on the east stub, three buggies and balls in the riverbed.

- **`pgc_harbour` — Harbour.** A 200 m harbour: a quay 4 m over a sunken basin (a lower floor painted as water; nothing simulates water), two slipways out of it (4 m over 22, about 10 degrees) so whoever is thrown in can walk back, three 50 m timber piers with T-heads on pilings (3.6 m of headroom under each deck, so the basin floor runs underneath), and a mole out to a lighthouse. Two small boats lie 1.5 m off a pier's pilings with their decks 1 m under it (a hop down and a jump back; the 1.5 m keeps the gap from being a slot), and a freighter lies along the mole with two containers, a bridge house and a gangway. The lighthouse is a 6 m core in bands, climbed by a square spiral: seven `bridge()` sides 3 m up over 6 (27 degrees) between 3 m corner landings, and at 21 m a gallery round three sides, 2.5 m over the lane below it, with barrels on it to throw off. Two quay cranes with a container hanging off each boom, stacked containers, a harbour master's office with a button-wired door, bollards, lamps, two buggies, cargo on the piers and balls in the basin. The survey passed it on the first run; the render moved the gallery's barrels off the side its camera stands on.

- **`pgc_bowl` — Fling Bowl.** A 96 m bowl: a 28 m sand floor with a 1 m pedestal in the middle (an octagon of two squares, a jump), 30-degree sides 15 m high, an 8 m rim walkway with a 1.5 m parapet, and a diving board 8 m out over the north slope to be thrown from. **The sides are a frustum of a 24-sided pyramid, one `bridge()` per side, each as wide as its side at the rim**, so neighbours overlap toward the middle; where they do, the facet whose own axis is nearer is the higher, so the surface is exactly the facet and the seams meet with no lip and no gap. Each slab is thick enough that its underside is under the ground at the rim, so there is no hollow under the bowl. **The survey found sixteen slots that are not there**: it reads a tilted box as its world AABB, and at the natural width the AABBs of two facets some way apart, or a facet and a rim block, came 0.06 to 0.65 m apart. Every pair clears from 1.2 m wider; the facets are 2 m wider, hidden under their neighbours either way. Boulders, balls and low-friction sleds wait on the rim. **Measured** with each pushed inward at 3 m/s: a boulder reaches the floor in 5.2 s and stops against the pedestal, a ball in 5.5 s, and a sled in 4.5 s, coming to rest 4 m from the middle; none climbs the far side. The render made the pedestal red and the floor sand: crates on an orange pedestal, and a floor the colour of the white facets, both disappeared.

- **`pgc_obstacle` — Obstacle Park.** A 160 m field with a course that starts at a pad: four 1 m steps (each a jump), six 2 m stepping stones 2.5 m apart in a zigzag at 4 m, a 0.8 m balance beam 12 m long, a landing, five blocks each 0.8 m higher and 2 m apart, and a platform at 8 m with a wall across it whose door is wired to a button on each side. Past the gate an 8 m tower 20 m tall is climbed by fifteen ledges spiralling round it, each 0.8 m up and 2.25 m on from the last (a loop is 6.4 m, so every ledge has headroom), the last level with the top: a deck to be thrown from into the sand pit, with crates and a ball on it and a button that opens the vault by the start (beach balls, a big die, a crate). Every jump is sized against the player's: 4.75 m level, 3.68 m up 0.8, 3.23 m up 1.0, and nothing asks for more than 2.5 m. **Measured with a bot driven box to box** (`headless_playground`'s route-driving rules, the gate opened by its button): with a 4 m landing it missed the first block every time on its first try, at 2 m and at 1.5 m alike, because turning off the beam left it 2 m to get up to speed; the landing is 6 m now and the course 2.5 m further west, and the bot makes all 33 jumps first time, start to tower top in 3,816 ticks (29.8 s). A fall lands in the field, which leads back to the start.

- **`pgc_nature` — Nature.** The first map made of materials (`mat()` in the builder; a box's `material`, game-playground's *Map materials*). A 220 m grass meadow with a snow field and a 30 x 20 m ice rink, a mud pit, a sand beach, and a stone-rimmed pond of water. The pond is entered over a 3 m landing on the rim with a ramp each side; two ramps meeting the rim straight left a 0.7 m slot. There is a rock-rimmed lava pit with a rock bridge across it, its access ramps flush with the bridge (0.25 m short was a slot), plus three rubber trampolines, a rock outcrop with a wooden lookout, and a glass greenhouse on a wood frame. Measured in game-playground's suite: ice keeps 91% of a slide against grass's 34%, mud slows a run to 2.3 m/s, rubber jumps 3.7 m, lava sends a player back to the spawn and melts a crate.

All of them are surveyed by `game-playground`'s `headless_playground` (*the hand-built maps, surveyed*: no slot, nothing unreached but what the document declares, no trap) and load through the catalogue on a dedicated server (`dedicated`'s map commands: `map pgc_quarry`). `game-playground/tools/screenshot.sh pgc_plots` / `pgc_quarry` renders them.

## Delivering it (not done yet)

A client builds the map too, so this is a **`dependencies`** pack, not `server_dependencies`: every client fetches it in the same content sync as the game. Both ends then add `<mount prefix>/maps` — the server from the game manager's dependency keys (`PlaygroundModule._add_delivered_maps`), the client from `DotClientLink.content_extra` (`PlaygroundClient._ready`). To ship: a `content/playground_maps/pack.json` in dot-server-deploy (`tmc/playground_maps`, `exclude_dirs: ["tools"]`), `dependencies: [tmc/playground_maps@0.1.0]` in `content/playground/game.yml`, publish both, and a client shell with this game-playground.
